1. Eligibility

1.1 Age

  • Minimum age requirements for solo, unaccompanied home visits to a beneficiary: 21 years.
  • MaiHoonNa does not prescribe any maximum age limit for enrolment as a Saathi. Retired individuals and senior citizens are welcome to participate, subject to satisfactory completion of the enrolment process and compliance with applicable eligibility requirements. In lieu of any age-based restriction, applicants shall complete a self-declaration regarding their physical fitness, health status, and any limitations that may affect service delivery. Such declaration shall be reviewed by MaiHoonNa's Operations Team at the time of enrolment, and MaiHoonNa reserves the right to determine, in its sole discretion, the suitability of an applicant for the role.
  • Applicants between 18–20 of age may participate through a Campus/Group track and require valid written consent from the parent or guardian as part of enrolment process.

1.2 Other Eligibility Requirements

  • Indian resident with a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar and/or PAN) for identity and background verification.
  • Provide valid current address proof and at least one personal (non-family) reference.
  • Reside within, or be able to regularly travel to, the designated MaiHoonNa service area/ geography.
  • Successful completion of Background Verification (BGV), the Saathi orientation, and the Empathy & Situational Judgment screening before being assigned to any Beneficiary.

2. Nature of the Relationship

2.1 Voluntary Service: Participation in the Saathi Network is purely voluntary, honorary community service undertaken out of civic duty and empathy. Nothing in these Terms shall be construed as creating an employer-employee relationship, contract of employment, agency, partnership, or any form of worker status between MaiHoonNa and the Saathi.

2.2 No Employment Benefits: Saathis acknowledge that their participation is voluntary and does not entitle them to any salary, wages, minimum wage protections, bonus, gratuity, provident fund (EPF), ESIC, or any other employment-related or statutory benefits.

2.3 Flexibility of Participation: Saathis retain complete discretion over their availability and participation. There are no mandatory minimum service hours, fixed schedules, or exclusivity obligations, and Saathis are free to accept or decline opportunities at their sole discretion.

3. Saathi Credits

Saathis earn Credits for verified companionship hours logged through the MaiHoonNa app, in accordance with the separate Saathi Credits Policy. The following principles apply:

  • Credits accrue only for hours logged and verified through the app's check-in/check-out (or approved manual override) process — the same encounter-tracking mechanism used for Care Companions.
  • Credits are a recognition and engagement mechanism only and do not constitute monetary compensation, wages, or remuneration for services rendered.
  • Credits carry no fixed cash exchange value, cannot be converted into liquid currency, and cannot be transferred or assigned. Redemption options are governed strictly by the separate Saathi Credits Policy.
  • MaiHoonNa reserves the right to modify the Credits structure with reasonable advance notice to active Saathis.
  • Credits are personal to the Saathi, non-transferable, and may be forfeited on termination for cause (Section 9).

  • All applicants must sign the BGV Consent Form and complete identity, address, and reference verification as part of the onboarding process.
  • Background verification shall be conducted through MaiHoonNa's empanelled verification agency, and the results shall remain confidential and may not be disclosed to the applicant.

  • Saathis further agree to undergo periodic re-verification and updated screening at such intervals as may be prescribed by MaiHoonNa from time to time.

5. Code of Conduct

Every Saathi is expected to uphold the following standards at all times during any MaiHoonNa-arranged interaction: Failure to adhere to these standards may result in suspension or termination from the Saathi Network.

Principle

What It Means in Practice

Dignity

Treat every beneficiary with the same respect you would give a loved elder in your own family. Address them respectfully and by their preferred name or title.

Presence

Be fully present during a visit — no phone use, personal errands, or distractions beyond genuine emergencies. Stay for the agreed duration; do not leave a beneficiary alone in a public place without informing MaiHoonNa.

Patience

Match the beneficiary’s pace. Never rush, sigh, or pressure them to “cover more” in a visit than they are comfortable with.

Financial Boundaries

Never accept cash, gifts over ₹100 in value, or any form of payment, loan, or financial favour from a beneficiary or their family.

Confidentiality

All information about a beneficiary — health, family, financial, or personal — is strictly confidential. Never post about a beneficiary, a visit, or case details on personal social media, with or without identifying details.

Physical Boundaries

No unsolicited touch beyond what is needed to safely assist mobility (e.g. an arm on stairs). No physical contact that could reasonably make a beneficiary uncomfortable.

Non-Discrimination

Provide the same quality of companionship regardless of a beneficiary's religion, caste, gender, or economic status. No religious or political preaching.

Punctuality & Reliability

Arrive within the scheduled window. If running late, notify the beneficiary/family and MaiHoonNa as soon as you know.

Safety Reporting

Report any safeguarding concern (signs of abuse, neglect, or exploitation) to your Saathi Coordinator the same day. Report any medical emergency to the Emergency Response Coordinator (ERC) immediately and stay with the beneficiary until help arrives, where safe to do so.

No Medical Advice

Saathis are companions, not clinicians. Do not suggest medicines, comment on treatment, or provide medical opinions — note observations and inform the family or Saathi Coordinator instead.

Same-Gender Matching

All Saathi-beneficiary pairings are same-gender, without exception.

Identification

Present a government-approved ID card (Aadhaar, driving licence, etc.) when requested.

Honest Records

Log check-in/check-out and any notes accurately. Falsifying a visit record or hours logged is treated as a serious violation (Section 9).

6. Safety Protocols

  • No overnight stays or multi-day arrangements are part of the Saathi Network scope at launch; any exception requires explicit prior written approval from MaiHoonNa Operations.
  • Saathis must check in and check out via the app for every visit; manual entry requires a stated reason.
  • If a beneficiary's home or the immediate environment feels unsafe, the Saathi should exit politely and contact their Saathi Coordinator immediately — MaiHoonNa will support the Saathi's decision to do so.
  • Saathis are not authorised to drive beneficiaries in a personal vehicle.

7. Training & Orientation

Before being assigned to a beneficiary, every Saathi must successfully complete the mandatory onboarding process, including the Saathi Orientation Programme covering the Code of Conduct, empathy and safeguarding principles, and app usage; pass the Empathy & Situational Judgment Screening; undergo and clear the required Background Verification (BGV) checks; and obtain formal approval from the designated Saathi Coordinator. No beneficiary assignment shall be made unless all such requirements have been satisfactorily fulfilled.

8. Grievance & Protection — Both Directions

MaiHoonNa maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy towards any form of misconduct, abuse, harassment, exploitation, discrimination, inappropriate behaviour, or violation of safeguarding standards by either a Saathi or a Beneficiary. All interactions must be conducted with dignity, respect, professionalism, and mutual trust. Any complaint, concern, or allegation of misconduct may be investigated by MaiHoonNa, and may result in immediate suspension, termination of participation, removal from the platform, and, where appropriate, reporting to the relevant authorities.

.

8.1 If a Saathi experiences misconduct from a beneficiary or family member

  • If a Saathi experiences or reasonably perceives any misconduct, harassment, abuse, threat, discrimination, or unsafe behaviour from a Beneficiary or their family members, the Saathi may immediately end the visit or interaction without penalty and is under no obligation to continue.
  • The Saathi must report the incident to the designated Saathi Coordinator as soon as reasonably practicable, preferably on the same day. MaiHoonNa shall promptly review and investigate the complaint in accordance with its safeguarding procedures.
  • Where misconduct is substantiated, MaiHoonNa may terminate the beneficiary arrangement and take any other appropriate action. Under no circumstances will a Saathi be required or expected to return to a situation that has been assessed as unsafe.

8.2 If a beneficiary or family experiences a concern about a Saathi

  • Reporting of Concerns: If a Beneficiary or their family members have any concern, complaint, or allegation regarding the conduct of a Saathi, the matter must be reported to MaiHoonNa Operations promptly for review and appropriate action.
  • Investigation Process: Upon receiving a complaint, MaiHoonNa shall conduct a fair, impartial, and timely investigation in accordance with its safeguarding and grievance-handling procedures, while ensuring confidentiality to the extent reasonably practicable.
  • Interim and Final Action: Where the nature of the complaint warrants, the concerned Saathi may be temporarily suspended from assignments pending investigation. If misconduct is substantiated, MaiHoonNa may terminate the Saathi’s participation in the programme and, where required by law or the seriousness of the conduct, report the matter to the appropriate law enforcement or regulatory authorities.

9. Suspension & Termination

9.1 Voluntary Exit: A Saathi may voluntarily pause or discontinue participation in the Programme at any time by providing notice to their designated Saathi Coordinator. Participation in the Programme is entirely voluntary, there is no minimum service commitment, and a Saathi shall not incur any penalty, forfeiture, or adverse consequence solely for choosing to withdraw from or pause participation.

9.2 Termination for Cause: MaiHoonNa may suspend or terminate a Saathi's participation immediately for:

  • Falsifying visit records, check-in/check-out data, or hours logged.
  • Any financial boundary violation (Section 5).
  • Verified misconduct, harassment, or abuse toward a beneficiary or family member.
  • Failure to report a known safeguarding concern.
  • Breach of confidentiality, including unauthorised social media posts about a beneficiary.
  • Providing false information during enrolment or BGV.

MaiHoonNa may temporarily suspend a Saathi's participation while any complaint, safeguarding concern, or alleged violation is being investigated. Such suspension is a precautionary measure and does not constitute a finding of wrongdoing.

Upon suspension or termination, the Saathi shall immediately cease representing themselves as a MaiHoonNa Saathi, stop undertaking visits arranged through the Programme, and return or securely delete any beneficiary information, records, identification materials, or Programme-related resources in their possession, subject to applicable legal requirements.
