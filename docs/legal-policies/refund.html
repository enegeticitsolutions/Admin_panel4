Refund Policy

1. Renewal & Cancellation

1.1 Subscriptions do not auto-renew. The Subscriber must actively renew before the expiry of the current Subscription period via the App.

1.2 The Subscriber may cancel the Subscription at any time by submitting a written cancellation request via care@maihoonna.com.

2. Refund Calculation & Processing

2.1 Refunds shall be calculated as follows:

  • Cancellation within 7 days of activation and before the first Mitra visit: full refund, less payment gateway charges.
  • Cancellation after first Mitra visit but before 50% of Hours consumed: refund of the value of unused Hours at the per-hour rate of the package, less 10% administrative charge and benefits consumed.
  • Cancellation after 50% or more of Hours consumed: no refund.
  • Starter package: no refund once any Hours are consumed.

2.2 Refunds, where applicable, shall be processed to the original payment instrument within 7–10 business days of the cancellation confirmation.

3. Termination

3.1 MaiHoonNa reserves the right to terminate the Subscription with 7 days' written notice in the event of a material breach of these T&C by the Subscriber, NOK, or any person at the Beneficiary's residence.

3.2 MaiHoonNa may terminate the Subscription immediately, without notice, if: (a) the Beneficiary is found to be suffering from an undisclosed communicable disease; (b) any member of the Subscriber's household physically threatens or harms a Care Mitra; or (c) the Subscriber engages in fraudulent or illegal activity in connection with the Subscription.
