1. Information We Collect

To provide safe and personalized elder care services, MaiHoonNa collects information including:

  • Account Information: Full name, phone number, email address, and age and date of birth of subscribers and beneficiaries.
  • Service Addresses: House/flat number, landmark, area, city, State and pincode for accurate companion dispatch.
  • Emergency Contacts: Names, relationships, and emergency phone numbers designated by family members.
  • Health & Activity Notes: Daily wellness check-in summaries, visit feedback, and activity logs.

This information is used solely for service delivery, visit scheduling, and emergency response.

2. How We Use Your Data

We process personal data only for lawful purposes related to the provision of our services, including:

  • Authenticate your login and verify mobile OTPs securely without requiring passwords.
  • Coordinate, dispatch, and track Care Mitra senior visits.
  • Deliver real-time visit reports and emergency SOS notifications to designated family members.
  • Maintain safety and service compliance across all visits.

3. Privacy Protection & Non-Disclosure

MaiHoonNa is committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining the confidentiality of your personal information. We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or use personal data for advertising or third-party marketing purposes. Personal information is protected through appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, including secure transmission and restricted access on a need-to-know basis. We may share personal information only with your consent, with authorised service providers bound by confidentiality obligations, with emergency responders or healthcare providers where necessary to protect life, health, or safety, or where disclosure is required by applicable law, court order, or lawful governmental request.

Data is stored on secure cloud servers with end-to-end encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest.

4. Account & Data Deletion Rights

You retain complete ownership of your data. You may request profile updates, account closure, or deletion of personal data at any time through the mobile application or by contacting care@maihoonna.in. Upon verification of the request and subject to applicable legal retention requirements, MaiHoonNa will delete or anonymise the relevant personal data within a reasonable period.
